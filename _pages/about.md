<div style="font-size:80%;">
	<p><b>Experience</b></p>
	<p>Zachary McCaw is currently an Adjunct Instructor at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. Previously, he served as:</p>
	<ul>
		<li>Senior Member of Research Staff at Voleon, where he worked on time series representation and forecasting.</li>
		<li>Staff Machine Learning Scientist at Insitro, where he worked on statistical genetics and clinical machine learning applied to oncology and neurodegenerative disease.</li>
		<li>Data Scientist at Google, where he worked on machine learning for genetic discovery and causal inference for developer productivity.</li>
	</ul>
	<p>His research interests include biostatistics, machine learning, statistical genetics, and clinical trials.</p>
</div>


<p style="font-size:80%;"><b>Education</b></p>
<div style="font-size:80%;">
	<ul>
		<li>Zachary conducted postdoctoral research on cross-population fine-mapping with Professor <a href="https://www.finucanelab.org/">Hilary Finucane</a> at the Broad Institute.</li>
		<li>He completed his Ph.D. in Biostatistics with Professor <a href="https://www.hsph.harvard.edu/xihong-lin/">Xihong Lin</a> at the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health.</li>
		<li>His dissertation, <i>Transformation and Multivariate Methods for Improving Power in Genome-Wide Association Studies</i>, developed new approaches for improving power in GWAS.</li>
		<li>He received an <a href="https://grantome.com/grant/NIH/F31-HL140822-01">F31 National Research Service Award</a> from the NHLBI for “Innovations in Genome-Wide Association Studies Inspired by Obstructive Sleep Apnea.”</li>
		<li>He also received an Intramural Research Training Award from the NIEHS, where he studied respiratory genetics with Dr. Steven Kleeberger.</li>
		<li>He completed his B.S.P.H. in Biostatistics and B.S. in Quantitative Biology at UNC Chapel Hill.</li>
	</ul>
</div>
